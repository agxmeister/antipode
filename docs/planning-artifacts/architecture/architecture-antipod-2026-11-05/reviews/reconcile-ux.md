# Reconcile: UX documents vs Architecture Spine

**Verdict:** the spine covers the shape of the Telegram surface (outbox, callbacks, message map, Owner allow-list, one Review sequence at a time, 👀 before work) but leaves roughly twenty UX-required behaviors either unsupported by the FSM, contradicted by a Deferred item, or unspecified enough that an epic builder would invent them. The highest-impact gaps are: the Peek status line has no source; cancel/discard-undo/accept-undo/release-failed have no states or events; Demos over 50 MB and the preview-wake state are contradicted by Deferred; budget raise and amendment-restart collide with "contract on the Branch"; the Owner-pause and usage-window pause share one `paused` state; and the wake-from-sleep semantics are not what AD-18 (crash-only) covers.

Severity: **High** = an epic builder cannot implement the UX behavior from the spine without inventing architecture; **Medium** = implementable but two builders would make different choices; **Low** = a one-line addition to an AD or convention.

## 6. Summary of required spine changes (for the author)

1. **Peek** (S3, S4): define the status-line source and the visuals source during `implementing`.
2. **FSM additions** (S8, I6, I7, F8, F9, I10, I13): `cancel` from every non-terminal state; `discard_pending`/`undo_discard`; `accept_pending`/`undo_accept`; `merged_release_failed` + `retry_release`/`leave`; `in_review → implementing: amended`; bootstrap's `blocked` and Accept outcome; route budget exhaustion through Triage or state that the Coordinator writes the Stuck reason.
3. **Pause model** (S7, F10): separate Owner pause from usage-window pause (two states or a `pause_reason`), Project-level vs Task-level `/pause`, budget-counter exemption on resume.
4. **Budget and amendments vs contract on the Branch** (I9, I10): where `+2` and amended criteria are stored.
5. **Intake output** (I3, I4, I11, I2): list of instructions; `draft_revision`; how quick-answer taps update a draft; how a `question` answer rejoins the original message.
6. **Parking Lot** (S5): `promote_finding` command running Intake; finding status; Task origin link.
7. **Wake-from-sleep** (F1): `wake` event, monotonic clock for Role timeouts, update-age rules, once-per-Project notice.
8. **Outbox** (F4, F7, F3, F12): retry/poison policy, loud/silent by kind, `expires_at` semantics, idempotency.
9. **Demo over 50 MB / preview wake** (N5): resolve the contradiction with Deferred, in either document.
10. **Registration** (S1, N2, N1): `/register` as a Telegram command, global Owner id, privacy-mode/supergroup checks.
11. **Renderer font** (D1), **window-share** (S6), **caption limit** (D3), `degraded` reason (F5), round `cause` (F11): one line each.
